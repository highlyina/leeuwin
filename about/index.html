Hey there! I’m Georgina.

I’m a Brisbane-based graphic designer and soon-to-be Bachelor of Design graduate from QCAD, Griffith University.

I’m passionate about all things design, with a focus on branding, typography and web design. I enjoy bringing ideas to life through visual narratives that explore the tension between old and new, and between digital and physical mediums.

As a creative thinker and problem-solver, I strive to produce meaningful work that is grounded in research and collaboration. My goal as a designer is to help people understand the world, each other, and ourselves a little better.

When I’m not designing, you’ll find me sketching, going down random Wikipedia rabbit holes, impulse purchasing another houseplant or indulging in an oat milk latte.

If you’ve read this far — I’d love to connect with you!
