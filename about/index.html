About me

Hey there! I’m Georgina, a Brisbane-based designer and final-year Visual Communication Design student at QCAD, Griffith University.

I’m passionate about all things design, with a focus on branding, typography and digital experiences. I enjoy bringing ideas to life through visual narratives that explore the tension between old and new, and between digital and physical mediums.

As a creative thinker and problem-solver, I strive to produce meaningful work that is grounded in research and collaboration. My goal as a designer is to help people understand the world, each other, and ourselves a little better.

I’m currently open to work opportunities and would love to connect with fellow designers and industry professionals.
